About this questionnaire

Purpose. This tool helps you assess IAM-related choices for a product under the CRA. It walks through questions about how your product authenticates and authorises users, admins, devices, or cloud services, and maps your answers to applicable CRA Annex I requirements and illustrative optional reference frameworks (ETSI EN 303 645, NIST SP 800-63B, IEC 62443-4-2, ISO/IEC 27002, ISO/IEC 15408 Common Criteria).

What you get. A report with your answers, risk profile, and CRA requirements mapped to the framework columns you selected. The output structure is explained on the completion screen.

Framework mapping. The links from CRA rows to NIST SP 800-63B, Common Criteria, and other standards are project-authored cross-mappings built into this application. They are intended for discussion and gap analysis only, and are not official ENISA publications, EU legal texts, or endorsed crosswalks from any standards body. Do not rely on them as authoritative in a formal conformity assessment.
